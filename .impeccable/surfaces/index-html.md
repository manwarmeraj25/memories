# Surface brief — the archive page (`index.html`)

Scope: the whole site; it is one page. Visitor mode: **Experience**.
Audience, job, content and constraints: see `PRODUCT.md`. Unchanged.

## Direction contract

**THESIS.** The archive is one continuous silk plane threaded on a single
cord, and scrolling pulls it. Photographs gather toward the cord as you pass
them and open out as you arrive. It refuses the dark masonry grid this
category ships, and it refuses the bound album this project already tried and
rejected — no frames, no mounts, no object pretending to be furniture.

**OWN-WORLD.** Silk black `#080900` on deep shadow `#050506`; drawcord gold
`#D4AF37` as the only accent and always a control, never decoration; channel
graphite `#2B2E33`; gather slate `#4A5158`. In daylight the same cape in
undyed silk: ivory `#E9E5DD`, graphite channels, a darker gold. Gold appears
only as hairline rule with a small terminal node. Type is Archivo Narrow in
wide-tracked caps for display, Archivo for prose, Azeret Mono for data only —
dates, counts, positions — because those are measurement, not costume.
Photography fills the frame; the linework is drawn over it.

**STORY.** The visitor understands this is one person's years, held on a
single thread. They believe it is still being added to. They pull down
through it and stop where they want to look.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** An opening that names the archive, kept at the user's
request: the title in wide-tracked caps, who keeps it, the lede and the site
quote, a cord readout of the measure (held / years / span), and the whole
archive drifting past edge-on as a strip the scroll pulls. No eyebrow above
the heading and no stat-card row — the measure is a single line of mono
readout on the cord. The plane proper begins one viewport down, where the
newest photograph fills most of the frame. A gold hairline crosses it with one node
on the cord; the year sits in wide-tracked gold caps against the hairline; the
caption and date sit small and tracked beneath. The photographs on either side
are already gathered — compressed toward the cord, overlapped, turned a few
degrees like pleated cloth. The primary action is the photograph.

**FORM.** Drawcord transforming cape — dealt challenger, judged *declined*
against assigned grounded candidate 4 of 7 (the almanac field), and adopted by
the user over both the roll and the pick. Seed key `f18a46ec`, re-roll 1.

**FINISH.** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Raises carried from declined challengers

- **From the VU meter bridge:** every year threads on the identical cord, so
  years are read by sweeping across them, never by opening one.
- **From the blast manifesto:** the year is structural mass at full scale; on
  narrow screens the plane restacks rather than shrinking the type.
- **From the man-machine grid:** state lights the cord's rule rather than
  drawing a highlight box.

## Honest risk this build must beat

Gathering can make photographs unreadable — overlapped, rotated, scaled down.
The gather may only ever touch the periphery; the photograph being looked at
is always at full size and full legibility, and opening one is always one
click away.
